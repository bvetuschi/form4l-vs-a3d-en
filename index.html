HeyGears system limits

  • 1It is a lease system. The printer never becomes yours.
  • 2No iperammortamento. As a result you cannot use Italy's iperammortamento: the +180% super-depreciation on the value of an interconnected digital asset.
  • 3It is a closed system. It forces you to use its own resins, at an average price of €85 per litre for models.

Form 4L advantages

  • ✓It becomes yours with a small investment, equal to one yearly HeyGears lease fee.
  • ✓You can also pay for it with a lease without losing the iperammortamento benefits.
  • ✓Output: orthodontic models up to about 1,000 per day, plus 120 high-precision models per day.
  • ✓Open materials: the F4BL version prints biocompatible resins and about 40 resins from other manufacturers with the Open Material Mode licence.
  • ✓Post-processing scales to industrial size and adds only 6 minutes to the print.
Where the starting numbers come from
  • Form 4L: 32 models per plate, 10 minutes of printing, 5 minutes of operator time, 8.62 ml per model and resin at €445 per 5 L, from the Dental TechPro calculator. Investment €7,125 (Form 4L basic package), €18/h labour and a target of 60,000 models in 220 days, from the same calculator.
  • A3D: up to 720 clear aligner models per day per printer (HeyGears); the 2,000 models per day in the brochure are the 4-printer configuration with Resin Station. For the A3D the brochure gives 90 seconds for automatic part removal and 150 seconds for automatic resin refill, 4 minutes in total between one plate and the next. The 8 models per plate are a working value, to be confirmed with the manufacturer, and the 8.62 ml per model is the same as the Form 4L.
  • Formlabs resin volume discounts: 15% from 30 L, 20% from 60 L, 25% from 120 L, 30% from 240 L, 35% from 1,000 L, 40% from 2,000 L, 45% from 3,000 L. The tier depends on the litres in a single order. The calculation assumes one order with all the resin needed in a year for the target, which is the best case; with several orders the discount may be lower (in that case use the manual field). For the A3D the discount is a free field.
  • The 12-minute A3D print time is calculated to reach 720 models per day with 8 models per plate. With labour at 0 minutes the A3D is treated as fully automatic.
